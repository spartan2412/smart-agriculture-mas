# Table 1. Frozen dataset and evidence composition

This is a benchmark composition table, not a claim that every source is a live farm observation. The scenario population is fixed at six crops, 30 study sites, 23 districts, 5 states, and 300 historical contexts.

| Layer | Count | What is counted | Research role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frozen study registry | 30 | Admitted crop × district × season sites | Locks location-aligned study sites before scenario generation. |
| Canonical scenario benchmark | 300 | Aligned historical decision contexts | The fixed six-crop, 30-site test population. |
| First / second enrichment contract | 300 | Scenario capability and evidence contracts | Makes context availability and action restrictions explicit. |
| Third enrichment: regional plans | 1507 | Source-linked calendar, nutrient, IPM, and conditional operation plan items | Includes 607 conditional items across 229 scenarios. |
| Official operation recovery | 41 | Source-quoted operation records | Provides scope- and condition-bound operational evidence. |
| Scoped official RAG | 92 | Admitted official source documents | Metadata-gated corpus with 4582 indexed chunks. |
| Fourth enrichment: post-harvest | 700 | Conditional crop-scoped handling plan items | Paired with 14 exact-crop passages and 300 site contexts. |

All counts are regenerated from the listed source artifacts. The operation and post-harvest layers retain their documented field-condition boundaries; they do not authorize a farm-specific action.
